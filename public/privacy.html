Privacy Policy

Udini Periodic Table • Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview

Udini Periodic Table is an interactive 3D data visualization web application that renders structured dataset records in four interactive 3D spatial layouts (Table, Sphere, Double Helix, and Grid) using Three.js CSS3DRenderer.

2. Information We Access

When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (Google Identity Services), our application receives an authentication credential strictly to verify your session on the client side. We only use basic OpenID/profile authentication provided by Google Sign-In.

3. How We Use and Store Your Information

  • Client-Side Verification Only: Authentication is handled entirely within your browser session to grant access to the 3D visualization workspace.
  • No Data Persistence: We do not store, log, sell, or share your Google account details, email address, or personal profile information on any external server or database.
  • Third-Party Services: The application fetches read-only sample dataset records from a public Google Sheet for rendering the 3D tiles.

4. Data Security & Revoking Access

All communication occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections. You may revoke this application's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Udini Periodic Table application, please contact the developer at izzuddinfathi0@gmail.com.
